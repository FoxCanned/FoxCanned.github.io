Yihao Zhen 甄奕豪

Hi! I am currently a Research Assistant at GVC Lab, Greater Bay University, advised by Dr. Xiaodong Cun. I received my M.S. degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and my B.S. degree from Shandong University. During my postgraduate studies, I focused on object tracking, particularly vision-language tracking and multi-camera multi-target (MCMT) tracking. I am currently working on human-centric video generation and video world models.
